StarTag

Texas SPV on every Marketplace car

A Chrome extension that shows the Texas Standard Presumptive Value (SPV) of the vehicle on a Facebook Marketplace listing, next to the asking price.

Add to Chrome

What it shows

  • The SPV of the listing's vehicle, and how far the asking price is above or below it.
  • The model and engine of the VIN the value is for.
  • Listings without a VIN get an estimate from their year, make, model and text, with a confidence level. Type the VIN on the card for an exact value.
  • Similar variants of the model (drive, engine, trim) and their values, on request; click one to use it.
  • Mileage is taken into account for recent model years.

How it works

SPV values come from the TxDMV Standard Presumptive Value calculator (tools.txdmv.gov). For each listing you open, StarTag sends TxDMV a VIN and the listing's mileage, nothing else. It asks before the first lookup.

StarTag has no server, no account and no tracking; results are kept in your browser so a vehicle is not looked up twice. Details are in the privacy policy.

Good to know

Values are for information only; TxDMV's own calculator is the authoritative source.
